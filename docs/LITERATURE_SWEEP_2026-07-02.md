### ADD-0 (the framing SPINE — the deepest item; nearly missed) — mechanism evaluation is the methodologically-FORCED primary lens, and the pilot LICENSES saying so
Post-verdict, reframe the whole contribution: **when a domain's performance floor is noise-dominated,
evaluating an LLM-design claim by outcome (did performance improve?) is under-powered by construction, and
evaluating the mechanism (did the fed information change the authored CODE and the policy?) is the
methodologically-appropriate primary lens.** We do not *assert* this — the seed pilot *measures* that
performance is noise-dominated (σ_seed ≈ 3× the SESOI), which LICENSES the claim empirically. This elevates
the mechanism analysis from "a supplement" (its dossier framing) to "the correct primary instrument for this
class of question," and turns the null into a transferable methodological contribution (aligns with Okhrati's
methodology-as-contribution + critical-analysis-of-AI-modelling sensibility). Ready contribution sentence:
"Because we measure that per-seed performance variance dominates any reward-feedback effect, we argue — and
demonstrate — that mechanism-level evaluation (does the LLM *use* the fed information in the code it writes)
is the appropriate primary lens for LLM-design claims in noisy-RL domains, not outcome performance alone."

### ADD-2 — Name the seed-count decision with the precision-sizing literature [VERIFIED 2026-07-04]
**Maxwell, Kelley & Rausch 2008, "Sample Size Planning for Statistical Power and Accuracy in Parameter
Estimation," Annual Review of Psychology 59:537–563** [VERIFIED — SSRN 1141961 + Semantic Scholar]; companion
**Kelley & Rausch 2006, "Obtaining power or obtaining precision," Psychological Methods 11(4):363–385**
[VERIFIED — PubMed 12971200]; R implementation MBESS (`ss.aipe`). AIPE = choose n so the *expected CI
half-width* is sufficiently narrow — a precision criterion DISTINCT from power. This is EXACTLY our
"size the winner-seed count so the CI half-width < SESOI at the upper CI of pilot variance." Ready methods
sentence: "We size the winner-seed count by accuracy-in-parameter-estimation (Maxwell, Kelley & Rausch 2008):
n is chosen so the expected confidence-interval half-width on the per-seed IQM difference falls below the
pre-registered SESOI, evaluated at the upper confidence bound of the pilot-measured σ_D — a precision
criterion distinct from, and here more demanding than, power." Converts ~350 from ad-hoc arithmetic → named,

### ADD-3 — ⚠ CORRECTION (2026-07-04): ρ is a one-line METHODS note; the mechanism instruments (not ρ) carry the "upstream" question
DO NOT interpret ρ=−0.141 as "the rewards induce different policy dynamics." At n=15, SE(ρ)≈1/√12≈0.29 → ρ is
**indistinguishable from 0** (95% CI ≈ [−0.6, +0.4]). Interpreting it as mechanism evidence is an
Okhrati-catchable over-claim (he would say "you are interpreting noise"). The honest statements:
- **[methods note, one line]** "Common Random Numbers provided no variance reduction here (pairing correlation
  point estimate −0.14, not distinguishable from zero at the pilot's n=15); we therefore report σ_D directly
  and treat the seeds as effectively independent."
- **[the REAL upstream story — mechanism chapter, NOT via ρ]** The seed variance sets the **performance

  metric — our mechanism instruments locate exactly where." Supported by the instruments, not by ρ. (The
